About Pixel Press

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Pixel Press processes images in your browser and does not upload the images you select to a server.

What are RMSE and MAX ERROR?

These metrics measure the difference between the compressed image and the original: RMSE describes overall pixel error, while MAX ERROR is the largest difference in a single color channel. Lower values mean the sampled images are closer. To speed up calculation, images are reduced before comparison, so some details may be missed.

Is this tool free?

Yes! Pixel Press is completely free to use.

What happens to transparency when converting PNG to JPG?

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas become white after conversion.
